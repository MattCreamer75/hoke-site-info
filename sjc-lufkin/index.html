Emergency Procedures

  1. Emergency Evacuation orders and Severe Weather outbreaks are delivered via radio or cell phone to all departments.
  2. In the case of an Emergency Evacuation or Severe Weather, immediately stop work, shut down the equipment, and proceed to the Emergency Rally Point or Storm Shelter.
  3. Do not leave the facility until you have been accounted for by the Emergency Coordinator.
  4. Refer to the Facility Map for Evacuation Routes, Storm Shelter, and Emergency Rally Point locations.

PPE Safety

Footwear. Visitors, who are not SJ Employees and if approved by the Facility Manager, may be exempt from the Safety Toe Shoe requirement. Visitors’ footwear must be fully enclosed (closed heel, closed toe, closed top) and have heels no higher than 1 inch – leather uppers and slip resistant soles are preferred.

General Facility Rules

  1. All Contractors, Service Providers, and Visitors must sign in and out at the Plant Office.
  2. Smoking is permitted in designated areas only.
  3. Visitors must be escorted and must remain with their escort.
  4. Mobile equipment has the right-of-way at all times. Maintain eye contact with Powered Industrial Equipment Operators and assume they cannot see you.
  5. Read and follow directions on all signs. Including but not limited to LOTO Required areas, PIE Only Operation areas, Authorized Person Only areas, etc.
  6. Do not discard any materials on site without prior authorization.
  7. Do not pour any liquids down drains or onto the ground without prior authorization.
  8. Perform all tasks in compliance with Federal, State EHS Rules and Regulations, and Stella-Jones Policies Programs and Procedures.
  9. If you bring any chemicals on site, the SDS must be submitted to EHS Contact.
  10. SDSs are available for review upon request.
  11. Do not handle any chemicals or materials for which you have not had proper training.
  12. Report any incidents to a Stella-Jones Supervisor.
  13. Report any unsafe acts or conditions to a Stella-Jones Supervisor Immediately!

Stella-Jones is committed to Safety, Health, Improved Environment, Leading our Decisions (SHIELD)

Stella-Jones is committed to the Safety and Health of all employees, contractors, service providers, visitors, and the environment in which we operate. Follow all rules and instructions while visiting or working in our facility.
