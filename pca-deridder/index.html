Contractor Safety Orientation

PCA requires this online orientation before you can work at the DeRidder, LA mill. Do it on your phone or a computer before you show up. Tap any picture to make it bigger.

  • About 1 hour
  • Good for 1 year
  • Bring your certificate
Start PCA Sign-Up →
You must complete all 4 training modules:
  1. PCA Contractor Safety Orientation (video)
  2. PCA Contractor Safety Orientation Quiz
  3. Confined Space Flyer
  4. Excavation Policy
You are not done until all 4 say Complete.
Before you start
  • Use your own real email. It becomes your username, and PCA sends your login and renewal reminder there. Don't use a made-up "no.email" address.
  • Write down your password. You'll need it to get your certificate again or renew next year.
  • Set aside about an hour. The video alone is 30 minutes. Turn the volume up.

Part 1 · Sign up (one time)

  1. Tap Start PCA Sign-Up above, then tap Register.
  2. In the search box type hoke and tap Go. Check the box for Hoke Industrial.
    Pick Hoke Industrial, not your own company name and not "Other Company."
  3. Scroll down and tap Start.
  4. Fill in your info:
    • Your first and last name
    • Your email (this is your username)
    • Make a password and type it again in Confirm Password
    • Check Contractor
    • Phone is optional. For Job Title, type your job (welder, fitter, helper, etc.)
    • Check I'm not a robot at the bottom, then tap Next
  5. Check Packaging Corporation of America Facility, then tap Next.
  6. Make sure your name and email are right, then tap Complete Registration.

Part 2 · Take the training

Complete all 4 modules: the Contractor Safety Orientation video, the Contractor Safety Orientation Quiz, the Confined Space Flyer, and the Excavation Policy. They unlock in order, so finish each one before starting the next.

  1. Tap the menu button (☰) at the top left.
  2. Tap View My Training.
  3. Video: tap PCA Contractor Safety Orientation.
  4. Tap Launch and watch the whole video (about 30 minutes).
  5. When the video ends, check the box "I officially state that I have viewed and understood this video" and tap Done and Close.
  6. Quiz: tap PCA Contractor Safety Orientation Quiz, then Launch.
  7. Tap Begin.
    Heads up: 18 questions. You need 80% (15 right) to pass, and you only get one try on each question. Pay attention to the video.
  8. When you finish the last question, tap the X at the bottom to exit.
  9. Confined Space Flyer: tap Confined Space Flyer, read it, then tap Close at the top right.
  10. Excavation Policy: tap Excavation Policy, read it, then tap Close.
  11. Check the list: all 4 modules (video, quiz, Confined Space Flyer, Excavation Policy) must say Complete. If any one doesn't, go back and finish it.

Part 3 · Get your certificate

  1. Tap the printer icon on the right side, in the Actions column.
  2. Save it or screenshot it.
  3. Text a picture of it to Matt. You're not cleared for PCA until he has it.
  4. Bring it to the mill (on your phone or printed).

Need to get back in?

Go to the same sign-up link and log in with your email and password. Your training and certificate are saved there.

The orientation expires 1 year after you finish it. PCA emails you before it runs out, and you take it again the same way.

Questions? Reply to Matt's text.
