PCA DeRidder Mill
Contractor Safety Orientation

PCA requires this online orientation before you can work at the DeRidder, LA mill. Do it on your phone or a computer before you show up.

  • About 10 minutes
  • Good for 1 year
  • Bring your certificate
Start PCA Sign-Up → Step-by-step with pictures
Before you start
  • Use your own real email. It becomes your username, and PCA sends your login and renewal reminder there. Don't use a made-up "noemail" address.
  • Write down your password. You'll need it to get your certificate again or renew next year.
  • Use Chrome or Safari, and turn up the volume for the video.

Part 1 · Sign up (one time)

  1. Tap Start PCA Sign-Up above, then tap Register.
  2. In the search box type hoke and tap Go.
  3. Pick Hoke Industrial and tap Start.
    Pick Hoke Industrial, not your own company name and not "Other."
  4. Fill in your info:
    • First and last name
    • Your email (this is your username)
    • Make a password and type it twice
    • Job title: type your job (welder, fitter, helper, etc.)
    If it shows a list of roles, check the one that fits your work.
  5. Tap Next.
  6. Pick Packaging Corporation of America Facility and tap Next.
  7. Check that your info is right, then tap Complete Registration.

Part 2 · Take the training

  1. Tap View My Training.
  2. Under My Assigned Training, tap PCA Contractor Safety Orientation.
  3. Tap Launch on each item and finish it:
    • Safety orientation video
    • Safety quiz (you have to pass it)
    • Confined space flyer
  4. When an item finishes, check the box that says you completed it, then tap Done and Close.
  5. When everything is done, the status changes to Complete.

Part 3 · Get your certificate

  1. Tap the printer icon on the right side, in the Actions column.
  2. Save it or screenshot it.
  3. Text a picture of it to Matt. You're not cleared for PCA until he has it.
  4. Bring it to the mill (on your phone or printed).

Need to get back in?

Go to the same sign-up link and log in with your email and password. Your training and certificate are saved there.

The orientation expires 1 year after you finish it. PCA emails you before it runs out, and you take it again the same way.

Questions? Reply to Matt's text.
